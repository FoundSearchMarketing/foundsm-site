A finding from the team, most weeks. One thing we saw on real accounts, and what it meant for the business. The note itself is a few hundred words.

  • Week of Nov 2 · Paid media The accounts that eased off in October kept the revenue. Where we bought less of the cheap volume, the quarter’s revenue held. The dashboards were the last to agree.
  • Week of Oct 26 · Pipeline Brand demand fell, and the forecast finally matched the building. The week search for the brand dipped, sales stopped being surprised by a pipeline they had been defending.
  • Week of Oct 19 · Creative The line that named the risk beat the line that named the offer. Fewer clicks. More conversations the sales team wanted a second meeting for.

One finding a week, published when we see it. Short enough to read between meetings.

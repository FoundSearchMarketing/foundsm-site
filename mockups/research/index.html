How the pages connect

  • The homepage cover’s “Read the Brief” opens the November issue.
  • The issue page is the letter. The signup beside it is for the rest of that issue by email: the Loop note, her questions, and what she noticed. It does not sit in front of the letter.
  • Each homepage Signal opens a note. This mock writes one of them out. The others land on their place in the library.
  • “All Signals,” the footer, and the Research nav open the library. Issues are the top shelf. Signals are the list below.

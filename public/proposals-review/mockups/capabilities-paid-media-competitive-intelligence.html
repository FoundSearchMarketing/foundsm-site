Capabilities

Your competitors publish their strategy every day. Almost nobody reads it.

Every ad they run, every landing page they test, every auction they enter and every one they walk away from is visible if you look. Most advertisers check a competitor's homepage twice a year and call it research.

Let's talk about your competition

Competitive intelligence is only worth doing if it changes a decision.

A deck of competitor screenshots is a book report. What matters is whether it tells you where to raise a bid, what to test next, and which fight is not worth having.

What we watch

Where the click actually goes.

Form length, offer framing, page structure, and how many steps stand between an ad and a lead. Often the most useful thing in the whole exercise, and the most commonly skipped.

Auction share of voice

Who shows up, where, and how often.

Creative and offer movement

Impression share, overlap rate, and position tracked over time, so a competitor entering or exiting your category registers as a trend rather than as a bad month.

Landing pages and conversion paths

What they are saying, and what changed.

Brand keyword pressure

Ad copy, promotions, and positioning monitored across platforms. A competitor rewriting every headline in a week usually means something, and it is worth knowing what.

Research that ends in a decision

Findings land as changes to bids, budgets, negatives, and creative tests. If a piece of research cannot be traced to something we did differently, it was entertainment.

Some competitors are buying share at a price no rational business should match. Recognizing that early saves more money than winning the auction would have made.

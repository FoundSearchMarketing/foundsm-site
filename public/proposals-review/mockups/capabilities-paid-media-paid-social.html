PLACEMENT STRATEGY

Automatic placements spread budget across every surface a platform owns and report back one blended number. We decide where the ads run, build creative for each surface, and show you where the money actually went.

Feed, Reels, Stories, and Audience Network do not perform alike, and automatic placements average them into a single number that hides which one is working. We report and bid at the placement level so the difference is visible and actionable.

A vertical Reel is not a feed image rotated ninety degrees. Each placement gets assets built for how people actually consume that surface, including how much of the frame the interface covers.

Audience Network and equivalent extensions are cheap for a reason. They stay in the mix when they prove out against qualified leads, and come out when they do not, rather than riding along inside a default setting.

Where the budget went, not just what it cost. If a third of spend landed somewhere nobody would have chosen deliberately, that should be a visible fact rather than a discovery six months later.

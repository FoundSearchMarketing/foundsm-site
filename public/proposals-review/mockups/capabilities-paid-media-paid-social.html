Creative is the targeting now. The platform decides who sees the ad, and the creative decides who stops.

Platform delivery systems have absorbed most of the audience decisions that media buyers used to make by hand. What still moves performance is the quality of the first-party signal you give them and the volume of genuinely different creative you put in front of them.

Three platforms, three different jobs

Reddit, TikTok, Snapchat, and whatever launches next. These are tests with a defined budget, a defined read window, and a decision at the end. Early-adopter advantage is real and it is temporary, so the discipline is knowing when a channel has earned a permanent line in the budget and when it has not.

Meta

The strategy that works on Meta will waste your money on LinkedIn.

LinkedIn

Volume, cheap reach, and a delivery system that increasingly decides targeting for you. The work is upstream: clean conversion signal through the Conversions API, first-party audiences worth modeling from, and enough creative variety that the system has something to optimize between. Optimizing toward raw leads here fills the funnel with people who never answer the phone.

Emerging

Expensive impressions and the most precise professional targeting available, which makes waste costly and precision valuable. Worth it when contract values justify the cost per lead. We run it against qualified-lead signal from the CRM rather than platform-reported conversions, because at this volume the platform's numbers are too thin to steer by.

Placement is a decision, not a checkbox

Audience Network and equivalent extensions are cheap for a reason. They stay in the mix when they prove out against qualified leads, and come out when they do not, rather than riding along inside a default setting.

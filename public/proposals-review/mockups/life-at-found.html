What it is actually like to work here

DAY TO DAY

Senior people, small teams, and direct client relationships from early on.

Account teams own the relationship and the results. You are in the room where budgets and priorities get decided, not relaying notes from it.

Paid media, data engineering, analytics and creative sit together. You learn how the pieces connect because the person who built the other piece is at the next desk, or in the next Slack channel.

Some of us are in the Fishers office every day, some split the week between home and the office, and some are fully remote across the US. We hire for the work, not for the desk.

Generous PTO, strong healthcare options and a retirement match, plus budgets for certifications, training and conferences. You do not have to negotiate for the things that make you better at the job.

Traditions we actually keep

Committees, food, celebrations, and the people who stay.

Volunteer, event and social committees own the calendar.

Quarterly outings or remote team building, and drives all year: a Humane Society supply drive, a children's book drive, a blood drive, and six years of Angel Tree gifts at the holidays. Remote teammates get a half day to volunteer where they live.

Food shows up every week, and the calendar has its own holidays.

Weekly food in the office, DoorDash credits for agency-wide meetings so remote teammates eat too, and a monthly brunch club. Then the big ones: Appetizer Day, the annual chili and soup cook-offs, and Foundsgiving.

Weddings, babies, new homes, degrees.

The team shows up for each other's milestones, and work anniversaries come with a gift budget so you pick something you actually want.
